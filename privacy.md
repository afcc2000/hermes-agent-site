# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy describes how **hermes-agent** ("the app") handles information when you sign in with your Google Account.

## Information the app accesses

The app only accesses the Google user data covered by the permissions (scopes) you explicitly approve on the Google consent screen. You can review these permissions before granting access.

## How the information is used

Google user data is used solely to provide the features you request from the assistant (for example, reading, searching or organizing your own content). The app does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not use it to train or improve generalized AI or machine learning models.

## Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data and does not share it with third parties, except when necessary to provide a feature you requested, to comply with applicable law, or to protect security.

## Storage and retention

Data is processed only for as long as needed to complete your request. OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to call Google APIs on your behalf.

## Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation, the app can no longer access your Google data.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
